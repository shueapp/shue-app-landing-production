SMS messaging & consent

How Shue uses text messaging, and your choices.

Shue may send SMS text messages to users who provide their mobile number and opt in. By providing your phone number and checking the consent box during sign-up or checkout, you agree to receive account and transaction messages from Shue at the number provided.

  • Program description: Account verification codes, order and shipping updates, auction and bid alerts, and raffle results.
  • How to opt in: Users provide their mobile number and give express consent via a checkbox during account sign-up or checkout. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your account activity.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from messages. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below.
  • Privacy: Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
