Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how ShueApp ("ShueApp," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our sneaker marketplace and related services (the "Service").

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, username, email address, and profile details you provide when you register.
  • Mobile phone number — provided by you if you opt in to SMS text messaging.
  • Transaction information — listings, orders, bids, raffle entries, and preorders you create or participate in.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as device and log information used to operate and secure the Service.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service.
  • To process transactions and communicate about listings, orders, auctions, raffles, and preorders.
  • To send account and transactional SMS messages to users who opt in (see below).
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Service and prevent fraud.

SMS / mobile messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, we may send you account and transactional text messages such as verification codes, order and shipping updates, auction and bid alerts, and raffle results.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories mentioned above exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Message frequency varies based on your account activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time; reply HELP for help.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate the Service (for example, with service providers who process payments or deliver messages on our behalf) or as required by law. As stated above, mobile opt-in and consent data are never shared for marketing purposes.

Data retention & security

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.

Your choices

  • You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may request access to or deletion of your account information by contacting us.

Contact us
